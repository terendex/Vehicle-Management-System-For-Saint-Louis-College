1. WHAT THIS IS

   This software is the on-site half of Saint Louis College's Smart Parking
   and Vehicle Verification System. It is internal college software, provided
   for installation on college-owned or college-authorised machines only. It
   is not offered to the public and carries no warranty.


2. WHAT IT CONNECTS TO

   Installing this does not create a separate system. This machine joins an
   existing deployment: it reads and writes the same live database and the
   same file storage as the college's cloud instance. Anything recorded here
   is recorded for the whole system, immediately.

   Because of that, the credentials entered during setup - the signing key and
   the database connection string - grant full access to live records. Treat
   them as you would any other production credential.


3. PERSONAL DATA

   In normal operation this software captures vehicle images and licence
   plates at campus gates and links them to registered students, employees,
   fetchers and visitors. That is personal information about identifiable
   people, and its handling is subject to the Philippine Data Privacy Act of
   2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and to the college's own privacy policy.

   Installing and operating this system is an act of processing that data.
   The operator is responsible for ensuring it happens under the college's
   registered privacy notice, retention schedule and access controls.


4. CAMERAS AND RECORDING

   This machine opens video streams from cameras on the campus network.
   Signage, consent and camera placement remain the college's responsibility
   and are not configured by this installer.


5. NO WARRANTY

   The software is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind, express or
   implied. It performs automated licence plate recognition, which is not
   perfect: it misreads plates, and it can fail to detect a vehicle entirely.
   Decisions that affect a person - denying entry, recording a violation -
   should be reviewable by a human being, and the system records its evidence
   so that they can be.

   The authors and the college accept no liability for loss or damage arising
   from use of this software.


6. ACCEPTANCE

   Installing this software means the installing administrator has authority
   from Saint Louis College to deploy it on this machine and to connect it to
   the college's live system.
